=== PAGE 1 ===
Day one at Northwind Labs begins at 09:00 at the Reception desk on floor 2. Bring a photo ID for check-in. Your badge is issued by Facilities before the security briefing. The briefing lasts thirty minutes and covers building access, emergency exits and visitor rules. After the briefing a Facilities coordinator walks you to your desk and explains the floor layout, the printer rooms and the quiet areas. Coffee and tea are available in the kitchenette on the same floor. If you arrive late, call the reception line and a coordinator will meet you at the lift. A temporary visitor pass is held at the desk until your photo ID has been recorded in the access system.

=== PAGE 2 ===
IT accounts are created from the HR start date. Your email alias is firstname.lastname@northwind.example. The temporary password expires after 72 hours and must be changed through the identity portal. Multi-factor enrolment happens in the same portal. The portal also lists the applications assigned to your role and lets you request access to shared drives. New joiners should sign in to the portal before the end of day one so that the account is fully provisioned overnight. If the alias you expect is already taken, IT appends a short numeric suffix and sends the final address to your personal email. Password reset links are valid for twenty minutes and can only be requested from a managed device.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Equipment: every engineer receives a 16-inch laptop and a noise-cancelling headset. Extra monitors must be requested from Facilities via the equipment ticket. Return all equipment within five business days of departure. Parking permits are arranged through Facilities as well. The standard laptop image includes the editor, the terminal and the company VPN client. Local administrator rights are not granted by default but can be requested through IT. A damaged device should be logged in the asset system before an exchange is issued. Headsets and docks are inventoried against your employee number, so keep the original accessories until the return window has closed.
