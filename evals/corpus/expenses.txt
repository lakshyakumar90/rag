=== PAGE 1 ===
Book flights through the travel portal at least 14 days in advance. Economy class is the default for trips under six hours. The preferred airline is Skyline Air. Rail travel is encouraged for journeys under three hours. The portal shows the lowest reasonable fare across approved carriers and records the reason when a more expensive option is selected. Last-minute bookings require approval from a director and must be explained in the expense report. Hotel bookings made outside the portal are still eligible for reimbursement if they meet the published limits. Frequent-flyer numbers can be added to your travel profile, but points earned on company travel remain with the employee. Changes to an itinerary should be made in the portal so that the travel team can assist if a flight is cancelled.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Reimbursement limits: hotel 180 USD per night in most cities, 240 USD in New York and San Francisco. Meals are capped at 60 USD per day with receipts. Client entertainment requires pre-approval from a director. Taxi and ride-share costs are reimbursed when public transport is not practical after 21:00. Laundry allowance applies only to trips longer than four nights. The corporate card should be used wherever it is accepted; personal cards are reimbursed only when the corporate card cannot be used. Currency conversion uses the rate on the day the charge appeared on the statement. Any amount above the published limits needs a written justification attached to the report before finance will approve it.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the trip. Receipts are required for any single item above 25 USD. Reimbursements are paid on the next biweekly payroll cycle. The finance team rejects reports without a cost centre. Each report must include the business purpose, the dates of travel and the project code where one has been assigned. Missing receipts delay the whole report, so upload them as soon as they are available rather than at the end of the quarter. An advance can be requested before a trip and is deducted from the first reimbursement afterwards. Finance publishes a short checklist each quarter covering the most common reasons for rejection. Questions about a rejected report should go to the finance help desk rather than to the traveller manager.
