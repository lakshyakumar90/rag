=== PAGE 1 ===
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are managed in the company password vault. Reuse of old passwords is blocked for the last 12 passwords. Shared accounts are prohibited under the access policy. The vault generates unique credentials for each service and records which account belongs to which owner. Passphrases are preferred over single words with substitutions, and dictionary words on their own are rejected by the vault policy. Access to the vault is reviewed every quarter by the service owners. If a vault entry is shared with a colleague for a planned handover, the entry must be rotated once the handover is complete. Password hints must never contain the password itself or a reference that reveals it to a casual reader.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems. Hardware keys are issued by IT; SMS codes are not accepted. Report a lost key to the security hotline within one hour. Visitors must be escorted at all times. A second key is kept in a sealed envelope at the reception desk for emergency access and may be issued only after identity verification. Authentication logs are retained for twelve months and reviewed after any suspected compromise. Applications that cannot support hardware keys must be placed behind the company single sign-on gateway. Security awareness training is assigned once a year and must be completed within thirty days of assignment.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Data classification levels are public, internal, confidential and restricted. Restricted files must be stored only in the approved encrypted drive. Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours to security@northwind.example. Quarterly phishing drills are mandatory. Public material may be shared without restriction, while internal material is for employees and contracted partners. Confidential documents require a named owner and an access list that is reviewed every six months. Restricted documents must never be sent by ordinary email, even to colleagues in the same office. The security team publishes a short guide each quarter showing how to classify a new document and how to request an exception.
