=== PAGE 1 ===
VPN: if the client fails at 40 percent, restart the service and reconnect using the gateway in Frankfurt. The gateway in Sydney is reserved for the APAC team. Split tunnelling is disabled by default. A fresh installation can be downloaded from the software catalogue on the intranet. Before opening a ticket, check that the client is running the latest version and that the device clock is correct, since an incorrect clock invalidates the certificate. Connection logs can be exported from the client menu and attached to the ticket. If several colleagues report the same failure at once, the issue is likely upstream and the service desk will post an update on the status page. Rebooting the device resolves roughly a third of reported connection failures.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Email: missing messages are usually caused by a full mailbox quota of 10 GB. Empty the deleted folder or request a quota increase from the service desk. Delivery rules can also hide messages in the archive. The archive is searchable from the same client and retains items for seven years. Shared mailboxes have a separate quota and are owned by a named team. If a message is stuck in the outbox, switch to offline mode and back again to force a resend. Spam filtering can be adjusted from the message context menu, but whole domains should only be blocked after consulting the service desk. Delivery receipts are disabled by default to reduce noise.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Laptop issues: contact the service desk at ext 4357 or desk@northwind.example. For a cracked screen, request an exchange rather than a repair. On-site visits take two business days. Loaner devices are available for multi-day repairs. The service desk is staffed from 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays and offers a limited weekend rotation for critical incidents. Before raising a ticket, note the asset tag, the operating system version and whether the problem is reproducible after a restart. Battery swelling is treated as urgent and should be reported immediately rather than queued with routine repairs. Software installations require approval through the catalogue, and the desk cannot install unlicensed tools.
