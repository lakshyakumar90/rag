=== PAGE 1 ===
Health coverage starts on the first day of the month following your start date. The standard plan covers 80 percent of specialist visits. Add a dependent within 30 days or wait for the next open enrollment. Dental and vision are included in the standard plan. Premiums for the standard plan are deducted from the first pay cheque of the month. A higher-coverage plan is available during open enrollment and carries a higher employee contribution. Claims are processed by the insurer within ten business days, and explanation-of-benefit letters appear in the member portal. If you move to a different country, contact the people team before the move to discuss whether your coverage can continue. Emergency treatment abroad is covered up to a stated annual limit.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Paid time off accrues at 1.75 days per month, capped at 25 days. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for primary caregivers and 8 weeks for secondary caregivers. Sick leave is tracked separately from paid time off. Unused paid time off does not carry over into the next calendar year unless local law requires it. Requests for more than five consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the leave portal. Managers are expected to respond within three business days. Public holidays are not deducted from the paid time off balance. Carers leave of up to five days per year is available for short-term family responsibilities and is recorded under a separate code.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Retirement: the company matches 5 percent of salary after one year of service. Contributions are vested immediately. The annual enrollment window closes on 15 December. Financial counselling sessions are available each quarter. The default investment fund is a diversified global index fund, and employees may switch funds at any time through the pension provider. Salary sacrifice contributions reduce taxable pay and are capped by local tax rules. Statements are issued every six months and include both employee and employer contributions. If you leave the company, your pension remains with the provider until you choose to transfer it. The people team runs a short webinar each November explaining the options before enrollment closes.
